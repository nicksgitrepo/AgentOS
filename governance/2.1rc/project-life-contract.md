# Project Life Contract

Status: `PREPARED_NOT_ACTIVATED`

The Project Life Contract answers one question before Bootstrap chooses
infrastructure:

> How real should the first project be, who may use it, what data may it hold,
> how long should it live, and who maintains or retires it?

It is one compact owner-intent object, not a ceremony. Bootstrap may use the
private-prototype default when discovery finds no material maturity, audience,
data, deployment, or lifetime signal. If a signal would change the route, it
asks one structured question and reuses the answer everywhere.

The contract records:

- maturity: concept, private prototype, limited product, beta, or production;
- assurance class and audience;
- source and account custody;
- real, durable, sensitive, or synthetic data posture;
- expected lifetime and maintenance posture;
- operating availability, scale, and support;
- commercialization, portability, and retirement;
- the production claim and explicit limitations.

`PRIVATE_PROTOTYPE` means owner-oriented validation, synthetic or explicitly
admitted data, no production claim, and campaign-bounded maintenance. A
`LIMITED_PRODUCT` is a real working product with a named audience, supported
scope, operating envelope, data posture, and rollback path. It is not silently
treated as standard production.

`HIGH_CONSEQUENCE_PRODUCTION` requires `HIGH_CONSEQUENCE` assurance. A
defaulted contract cannot claim production or sensitive durable data.

The compiled object is content-addressed by `life_contract_sha256` and is
bound into the exact creation plan and typed Project Context.
