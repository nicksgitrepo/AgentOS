# Boundary Contract

Status: `PREPARED_NOT_ACTIVATED`

The Boundary Contract makes the operating boundary executable. Every row says
what is constrained, who has authority, what happens automatically, what is
forbidden, what happens at the limit, what work remains unaffected, and what
evidence clears or changes the boundary.

It has four classes:

1. `CONSTITUTIONAL` — immutable AgentOS protections: no secrets, silent
   spending, impersonation, unauthorized destruction, publication by default,
   self-acceptance, out-of-custody deployment, hidden scope expansion,
   equivalent retry loops, imported-content execution authority, or removal of
   internal recovery.
2. `OWNER_SOVEREIGN` — the current owner’s intent and boundaries for outcome,
   audience, data, provider spending, source custody, licensing, promotion,
   maintenance, retirement, and destructive action.
3. `DERIVED_OPERATING` — limits calculated from the Project Life Contract,
   technical baseline, and Delivery Policy. These normally throttle or warn and
   do not interrupt unrelated work.
4. `TEMPORARY_PROBE` — read-only Bootstrap checks. Remote authentication,
   provider quota, publication, deployment, and rollback remain explicitly
   unrun owner boundaries.

The conflict rule is **more restrictive wins**. Project extensions may add
stricter rows but cannot weaken constitutional rules, owner authority, audit
independence, Runtime custody, or recovery.

A genuine boundary holds only its dependent outcome. Unaffected work continues.
The compiled object is content-addressed by `boundary_contract_sha256` and is
bound into the exact creation plan and typed Project Context.
